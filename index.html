    I am a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2024–Present). I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I completed my B.Eng. (2020–2024).
